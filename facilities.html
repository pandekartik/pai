Equipment

Our Machines

A comprehensive range of precision machining and manufacturing equipment to handle diverse production requirements.

CNC / VMC & Precision Machines

CNC Machine

Computer Numerical Control turning centre for high-precision external and internal turning, facing, and threading of cylindrical components with tight tolerances.

CNC Machine J 300 LM

Latest-generation CNC turning centre with enhanced axis travel and live tooling capability, enabling complex multi-operation parts in a single setup for improved cycle times.

VMC Machine

Vertical Machining Centre for high-accuracy milling, drilling, tapping, and contouring of prismatic components, delivering repeatable precision for complex part geometries.

VMC Machine 850V / 1000

Heavy-duty VMC with 850–1000mm table travel for larger workpieces, providing high-speed spindle performance and rigid structure for demanding milling and boring operations.

Surface Grinding Machine

Precision surface grinder for achieving fine flat surface finishes and tight dimensional tolerances on workpiece faces, ensuring parallelism and flatness to micron levels.

Grinding Machine

General-purpose grinding machine for cylindrical, internal, and profile grinding operations, used to achieve precise dimensional accuracy and superior surface quality.

Wire Cut Machine

Electrical discharge wire cutting (WEDM) machine for intricate profile cutting of hardened metals and tool steels, delivering burr-free edges with high dimensional accuracy.

Supporting Machines

Traub Machine

Automatic single-spindle turning machine for high-volume precision turned parts, ideal for producing small-diameter components with complex profiles efficiently.

Sliding Traub Machine

Sliding-head Traub lathe for Swiss-type precision turning of slender and small components, offering superior concentricity and surface finish for high-volume production runs.

Drill Machine

Heavy-duty pillar drill press for accurate hole making, reaming, and tapping operations across a variety of metals, supporting secondary drilling on machined components.

Vibro / Tumbling Machine

Vibratory finishing / tumbling machine for deburring, edge-breaking, and surface smoothing of batch components, ensuring consistent post-machining finish quality at scale.

Quality Control

Quality Inspection

We maintain rigorous quality control with advanced inspection instruments ensuring every component meets precise specifications.

Profile Projector

Optical comparator for magnified 2D profile measurement of component contours, thread forms, and complex geometries against master overlays.

Digital Height Gauge

High-accuracy digital height gauge for measuring and scribing precise heights, step dimensions, and perpendicularity of machined features on a surface plate.

Hardness Tester

Rockwell / Brinell hardness testing instrument for verifying material hardness values on heat-treated and raw components, ensuring correct material properties.

Roughness Tester

Surface roughness profilometer (Ra/Rz) for quantifying surface texture and finish quality of machined surfaces, confirming compliance with customer surface finish specs.

Instrument & Gauge Shelf

Organised calibrated gauge storage system including micrometers, vernier calipers, bore gauges, plug gauges, and ring gauges for in-process dimensional checks.

Trimos V4-700

Trimos V4-700 precision vertical measuring instrument for high-accuracy height, step, and comparative dimensional measurements with digital readout, traceable to national standards.
