Who We Are

Specialists in High-Precision
CNC & VMC Machining

Pandurang Auto Industries is an ISO 9001:2015 certified company specializing in high-precision machining. We are a custom CNC machined parts manufacturer delivering precision VMC services for a wide range of industries — serving customers with consistent quality and tight tolerances.

With over 23 years of experience, we specialize in Mass production and Batch production parts — helping customers move quickly from design to finished component, reliably and cost-effectively.
