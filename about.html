Who We Are

Precision Manufacturing You Can Trust

Pandurang Auto Industries is an ISO 9001:2015 certified company. Specialized in High precision machining facility. We are a custom CNC machined parts manufacturer specializing in precision VMC services for a wide range of industries.

With over 23 years of experience, our team delivers high-quality machined components with tight tolerances, reliable turnaround times, and exceptional attention to detail. We specialize in Mass production and Batch production parts, helping our customers move quickly from design to finished component.

Pandurang Auto Industries is committed to deliver precision, quality, and dependable service.
