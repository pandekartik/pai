About Us

ISO 9001:2015 Certified
Precision Machining

Pandurang Auto Industries is an ISO 9001:2015 certified company, specialized in high-precision CNC and VMC machining. We are a supplier of a variety of metal components with expertise in precision manufacturing, serving sectors like automotive, industrial products, oil and gas, electronics, and energy and power.

With over 23 years of experience, our team delivers high-quality machined components with tight tolerances, reliable turnaround times, and exceptional attention to detail — from our facility in MIDC Chinchwad, Pune.

Industries We Serve

We manufacture precision CNC and VMC machined components for a wide range of industries, delivering consistent quality and tight tolerances.

Automobile Industries

Precision machined components for the automotive sector — manufactured to exact tolerances for reliable, high-performance applications.

Hydraulic Applications

Expert manufacturing of tight tolerance hydraulic components — valves, cylinders and fittings machined for leak-free, high-pressure performance.

Pump & Valve Industries

CNC machined pump and valve components serving the oil and gas, water management, and process industries with precision-crafted parts.

Electronic Industries

Cylindrical barrels, bolts and precision housings from aluminum, copper and brass grades — machined to exacting specifications for electronics.

Industrial Products

Custom machined industrial components for a broad range of applications — from fixtures and fasteners to specialized structural parts.
