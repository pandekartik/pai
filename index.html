About Pandurang Auto Industries

ISO 9001:2015 Certified
Precision Machining

Pandurang Auto Industries is an ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturer specializing in high-precision CNC and VMC machining. Based in MIDC Chinchwad, Pune, we serve automotive, hydraulic, electronic, oil & gas, and energy sectors with consistently tight-tolerance components.

With over 23 years of hands-on experience, our team delivers mass and batch production parts with exceptional accuracy — helping customers move swiftly from design to finished component.
