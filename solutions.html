Industries We Serve

Pandurang Auto Industries brings over two decades of precision manufacturing expertise to serve a diverse range of industrial sectors. Our state-of-the-art CNC and VMC machining capabilities, combined with our ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management system, enable us to deliver components that meet the most exacting tolerances and standards.

From high-volume automotive components to specialised parts for the Oil & Gas and Energy sectors, we work closely with our clients to understand their requirements and deliver solutions that drive performance and reliability across every application.

Sector 01

Automobile Industries

The automotive sector demands the highest levels of dimensional accuracy, surface finish, and material integrity. PAI has been a trusted precision machining partner for leading automobile OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers, manufacturing critical components that directly affect vehicle performance and safety.

Our CNC and VMC machining centres operate to tight tolerances with full traceability, ensuring every part meets stringent quality standards before it reaches the assembly line. We work with a wide range of automotive-grade alloys and steels to suit diverse component requirements.

Key Applications
  • Engine components
  • Transmission parts
  • Suspension components
  • Brake system parts

Sector 02

Hydraulic Applications

Hydraulic systems operate under immense pressure and rely on leak-free, perfectly machined interfaces to function safely and efficiently. PAI manufactures precision components for hydraulic systems used across industrial machinery, construction equipment, and mobile hydraulics.

Our machining processes achieve the bore tolerances and surface finishes critical for sealing performance in high-pressure hydraulic environments. We work closely with hydraulic system designers to meet exact specifications and ensure long service life of all components.

Key Applications
  • Hydraulic cylinders
  • Valve bodies
  • Pump housings
  • Manifold blocks

Sector 03

Pump & Valve Industries

Pumps and valves form the backbone of fluid handling infrastructure across water treatment, chemical processing, and industrial manufacturing. PAI supplies precision machined components to pump and valve manufacturers who demand tight tolerances, superior surface finishes, and consistent dimensional repeatability.

With our multi-axis CNC capabilities, we produce complex geometries required for efficient fluid flow while maintaining the material properties necessary for resistance to corrosion and wear in demanding service environments.

Key Applications
  • Pump shafts
  • Valve stems
  • Impellers
  • Seal housings

PAI produces precision structural and mechanical components for electronic enclosures, connectors, and assembly hardware used in industrial electronics and control systems.

Our machining capabilities support the fine tolerances and smooth surface finishes needed for reliable electrical contacts and mechanical interfaces in electronic products, ensuring consistent performance across high-volume production runs.

  • Cylindrical barrels
  • Connector housings

Sector 07

Oil & Gas Industry

The oil and gas sector operates in some of the most extreme conditions encountered in any industry — high pressures, corrosive media, and remote or subsea environments where component failure is not an option. PAI manufactures precision components from high-performance alloys and stainless steels suited to these demanding operating conditions.

Our machined components for the oil and gas industry are produced with complete dimensional traceability, material certification, and thorough inspection documentation to meet the rigorous quality and safety standards required by upstream and downstream operators.
